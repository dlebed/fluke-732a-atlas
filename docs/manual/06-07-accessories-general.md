## Section 6 — Accessories (PDF p67, manual p6-1/6-2)

### 6-1. INTRODUCTION (PDF p67)
6-2. This section of the manual describes the accessories available for use with the model 732A.

### 6-3. DUAL MOUNTING FASTENER (M00-800-523) (PDF p67)
6-4. The Dual Mounting Fastener is a 8-32 threaded fastener designed for bolting two half-rack width instruments together. The Dual Mounting Fasteners may be used for either dual rack mounting applications (as used in the M07-200-603 Full-Width Rack Mount Kit) or dual table top applications. Four M00-800-523 fasteners are required for each pair of half-rack width instruments.

### 6-5. HALF-WIDTH RACK MOUNT KIT (M07-203-601) (PDF p67)
6-6. The Half-Width Rack Mounting kit permits the 732A to be rack mounted. A blank filler panel is supplied, allowing left or right hand offset mounting. Assembly instructions are supplied with the kit.

### 6-7. FULL-WIDTH RACK MOUNT KIT (M07-200-603) (PDF p67)
6-8. The Full Width Rack Mounting kit permits the 732A to be rack mounted side-by-side with another half rack width instrument. This rack mounting method requires the 732A to be bolted to the adjacent instrument. To facilitate bolting the instruments together, four M00-800-523, Dual Mounting Fasteners are included with the kit. Assembly instructions are supplied with the kit.

### 6-9. LOW THERMAL EMF CABLE ASSEMBLY (5440A-7002) (PDF p67)
6-10. The Low Thermal EMF Cable Assembly minimizes the effects of thermal emf errors in test and calibration set-ups. The plugs used are made of the same material as the jacks used in the instrument. Connections between the cables and plugs are carefully made to minimize generation of thermal errors.

### 6-11. BATTERY PACK (732A-7001) (PDF p67)
6-12. The Battery Pack is a replacement module for the rear panel, Battery Module on the 732A. It may be used as an additional auxiliary source, or as a spare.

### 6-13. TRANSIT CASE (732A-7002) (PDF p67)
6-14 The Transit Case provides a means of transporting the 732A while continuously powered by a battery source contained within the Transit Case. This allows continuity of standardization transportation over long distances.

### 6-15. BATTERY CHARGER (732A-7003) (PDF p67)
6-16. The Battery Charger provides the capability to charge up to four battery packs at once. This unit is designed to be used with the transit case for extended battery operation during transit.

This appendix contains information necessary to backdate the manual to conform with earlier pcb configurations. **OLDER INSTRUMENTS**
To backdate this manual to conform with earlier assembly revision levels, perform the changes indicated in Table 7A-1.

**CHANGES**
